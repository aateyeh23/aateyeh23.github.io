I am a second-year PhD student in Statistics at UC Berkeley.

I am interested in the theory of modern machine learning, especially what transformers and other sequence models can learn and compute (in-context and online learning, efficient long-context architectures); the optimization dynamics of training, such as step-size schedules; and causal inference and experimental design, including how AI predictions can make randomized trials more efficient.

See my research for papers and preprints, or my bio for more about me.
